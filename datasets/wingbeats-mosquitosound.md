# WINGBEATS / MosquitoSound

**Dataset ID:** `mosquitosound`  
**Year:** 2017 (source); 2026 archive  
**Modality:** optical wingbeat  
**Environment:** controlled insectary  
**Main tasks:** species classification

## Description

A large optical wingbeat dataset collected using infrared light modulation from flying mosquitoes in insectary conditions. The 279,566 examples comprise 6 species, single-channel sequences of 3,750 samples at 6 kHz. The source archive provides a random train/test split; samples are not necessarily independent biological individuals. **Not microphone audio.** WINGBEATS and MosquitoSound are related representations/distributions, not two independent datasets. Verify class labels and version when combining or citing.
