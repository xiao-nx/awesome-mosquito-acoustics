# HumBugDB

**Dataset ID:** `humbugdb2021`  
**Year:** 2021  
**Modality:** airborne audio microphone  
**Environment:** lab and field; 8 experiments  
**Main tasks:** detection; species classification

## Description

Original Zenodo v0.0.1: 71,286 seconds (~19.8 h) of labelled mosquito audio, plus 53,227 seconds (~14.8 h) corresponding background recordings, from eight experiments. Of the labelled mosquito audio, 64,843 seconds have species metadata for 36 species or species complexes. The dataset has audio multipart archives and CSV metadata, with code and documentation on the project GitHub. Device, country, sampling environment, and species are not uniformly distributed; group-aware testing is essential. Consider recording provenance and potentially related prior HumBug resources when merging.
