# Mosquitoes Bioacoustic Features

**Dataset ID:** `vasconcelos2020`  
**Year:** 2020  
**Modality:** airborne audio microphone  
**Environment:** laboratory, Madeira  
**Main tasks:** classification; feature extraction

## Description

Laboratory microphone recordings from Madeira, Portugal, collected using a microcontroller and mobile phone. The paper reports three mosquito taxonomic groups, including *Aedes aegypti*, *Culex pipiens/quinquefasciatus* grouping, and *Culiseta*; the exact distinctions among samples should be inspected in the files. Audio snippets are around 300 ms and come with 34 extracted acoustic features, with sample rates 8, 44.1, and 48 kHz. Suitable for signal/feature analysis and classification, with limited independent geographic coverage.
