# Code of Conduct

We aim to maintain a welcoming, inclusive, and technically rigorous research community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and evidence-based.
- Welcome questions and corrections, including from newcomers.
- Attribute original researchers and respect data/software licenses.
- Disagree with claims and methods, not with people.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discrimination, deliberate misinformation, and sharing private or restricted research data without permission are not welcome.

## Reporting

For now, use a GitHub issue for non-sensitive community concerns. **Do not disclose sensitive reports publicly.** Maintainers should add a private reporting email or GitHub private security/contact channel before publicly inviting sensitive reports.

Maintainers may moderate discussion and remove inappropriate contributions.
